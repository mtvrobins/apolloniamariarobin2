Welcome to Sakura

A Japanese restaurant on the Boulevard in Weston-super-Mare. Sashimi cut to order, sushi rolled by hand, crisp tempura, katsu curry and generous donburi, served beneath our cherry blossom in a dining room dressed with noren and lanterns. Dine in with us, or call ahead and collect.

The Morning's Catch

Sea bream, sea bass, mackerel and whole tuna loin come in fresh and are broken down in our kitchen, alongside yellowtail, scallop, sea urchin and bluefin toro.

What you see on the counter in the morning is on your plate that evening.
